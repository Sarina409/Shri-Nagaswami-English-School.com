Our Rich Legacy

Founded with a vision to bring quality education to every child, Shri Nagaswami English School stands as a beacon of academic excellence and holistic development. Our legacy is built on a foundation of dedication, integrity, and innovation, shaping future leaders for tomorrow.

Key Milestones

  • 2005: Establishment of Shri Nagaswami English School.
  • 2012: Introduction of advanced digital learning facilities.
  • 2018: Inauguration of Atal Tinkering Lab for innovation and creativity.
  • 2023: Recognized as a Center of Excellence for holistic education.

Our Commitment to Excellence

We continue to innovate and inspire with our unwavering commitment to excellence. Our goal is to empower students with the knowledge and skills to excel in all walks of life while fostering a sense of responsibility and global citizenship.
